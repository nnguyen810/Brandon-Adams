About Us

Security Professionals Established since 2009, Brandon Adam Security Services, Inc. is privately owned by a group of retired law-enforcement members. We are licensed by the State of New York Department of State and have affiliates throughout the world. Our command center and the corporate office is conveniently located at 2646 Route 112 Unit A Medford, Ny 11763 (New York) and operated 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We are licensed by the New York State Department of State Division of Licensing with multiple satellite offices throughout New York. Our current coverage locations include all five boroughs of New York City, Central Valley, Westchester, Rockland County and Long Island.
